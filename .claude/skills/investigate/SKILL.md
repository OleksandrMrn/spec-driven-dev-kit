name: investigate
description: Investigate an idea that has no ticket yet. Research what needs no decision, interview the user in rounds, record decisions, findings and assumptions in docs/investigations, and write the roadmap from which tickets are cut. Use only when invoked explicitly.
argument-hint: "<slug> [the idea in one sentence]"
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Investigate the idea given with the invocation (`$ARGUMENTS`: `<slug>` in snake_case and, on the first run, the idea in a sentence) until it is decided well enough to plan, and write its roadmap. The result is input for later specs, not an instruction for agents and not a license to implement anything (AGENTS.md section 0; `docs/investigations/README.md`).

1. Read `AGENTS.md` (sections 1, 5, 7 and 9) and `docs/investigations/README.md`. Find the folder `docs/investigations/<slug>/`. None → create it with `investigation.md` from `docs/investigations/_template/investigation.md`, `status: draft`, the slug, a title, the user as author and the date in the front matter, and the idea under "Idea" in the user's words; without an idea in the invocation, ask for it first. One → read `investigation.md` and, if it exists, `roadmap.md` in full, then act on the status. `draft` → continue; an answer the user wrote under a question in "Open questions" since the last run counts as one given in chat. `parked` → ask whether to resume; on yes set `status: draft` and the date, note it in "Log" and continue, otherwise stop. `ready` or `rejected` → stop and say so: an agreed roadmap continues in tickets (`docs/specs/README.md`: a spec when its triggers apply or the person asks for one), and either is reopened only by the user setting `status: draft`, after which new decisions supersede the old ones (`docs/investigations/README.md`, "Statuses"). Change nothing outside this folder: no code, no specs, no ADRs.
2. Research what needs no decision from the user (AGENTS.md section 1: what is already defined somewhere is looked up, not asked): the code, docs and assets the idea touches, if the repository has any; platforms, libraries, services, markets, prices and limits outside it. Record each result in "Findings" with its source and date, marked `verified` when read from the source and `unverified` when from memory or inference; say what could not be checked (no web access, no account) instead of guessing. For every external dependency the idea relies on (an API, a service, an exchange, a payment provider) fill its entry in "External dependencies" before the roadmap builds on it: the endpoints or features needed with their status and changelog, the access requirements (keys, whitelist, KYC, contract), the limits and fees, and what remains unknown. A blocker found there is raised in the next round as a decisive question, not deferred.
3. Ask a round of questions: only what the user alone can decide (intent, priorities, constraints, trade-offs). One block per round, up to 15 questions and no more than the open areas need, grouped by area, the decisive ones first: those whose answer could kill or reshape the idea. Each question `Q<n>` has one line on what in the roadmap depends on it; where options exist, two or three with a recommendation and the default taken if the user does not care. Take the areas from "Coverage": every area ends `covered` or `n/a` with the reason, and a round opens the areas that the earlier ones lead to. Do not ask what the findings, the decisions or an earlier round already answer. Write the questions into "Open questions" first, so the user can also answer in the file, then ask them in chat and wait for the answers.
4. Record the answers at once, before anything else: a decision into "Decisions" as `D<n>` with the question, the options, the choice, why and the date; a fact into "Findings"; "don't know" or "later" into "Assumptions" as `A<n>` with the roadmap step that will verify it; a risk the answers or findings expose into "Risks" with its cheapest check; a question the user cannot answer stays in "Open questions" with who answers it. A new decision that contradicts an earlier one is added and the earlier one marked superseded, never edited away. Update "Coverage" and add the round to "Log". Then research what the answers made researchable (step 2), derive the next round from the answers and the open areas, and go to step 3. Rounds end when no open question blocks the roadmap, or when the user says so.
5. Write `roadmap.md` from `docs/investigations/_template/roadmap.md`, or update it, from the decisions and the assumptions only; nothing still open appears in it as decided. Before writing it, re-read "Decisions" against "Coverage": a contradiction between decisions goes back to the user first. What the user left open does not block: an open area or question and an assumption with no verifying step each become the step that closes them (a spike, a question to a named person), listed under "Open points" at the top of the roadmap; the investigation stays `draft` while an area is open, since `ready` needs every area `covered` or `n/a`. The roadmap has phases with an exit criterion each and ordered steps `S<n>`, each with what is done, why, what it depends on, which decisions and assumptions it realizes and what is verified before the next step. For every entry in "External dependencies" the first step that touches it is one real operation end to end with real credentials, before anything is built on it; that step belongs to the roadmap and is not run by this skill. The cheapest check of each entry in "Risks" goes as early as it can. Close with "Candidate tickets", one paragraph per ticket with title, goal, the decisions it realizes and the tickets it depends on, ready to become tickets (`docs/specs/README.md`: a spec when its triggers apply, linking this folder in "Context"), and with "Out of the roadmap", what the decisions excluded.
6. Report the path of the folder, the decisions of this run, the assumptions, the open questions with who answers them and what could not be verified, then stop. Only the user sets `status: ready`, when the roadmap is agreed, and `parked` or `rejected`; this skill changes the status only from `parked` back to `draft`, on the user's confirmation (step 1). No commits.
